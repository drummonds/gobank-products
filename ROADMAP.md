# Roadmap

Named epics, each item an open issue labelled `roadmap` on the Bytestone
Forgejo (task-plus `tp roadmap`). An item leaves the file when its issue
is closed and lands in the changelog under the version that finished it.

## Contract

What a version carries beyond its declarations and rules.

- #5 Adoption and withdrawal: what a version carries for the bank's up and
  down migration beyond its declarations, if anything (gobank story 1.8.2)

## Versions

New rules in the v1 families, each a version of its own.

- #6 A version with a rule for `ParameterChange`: applying the accrual to
  date at a rate change, as some products do
- #7 Notice periods

## Engine

The day rule and the facts it reads.

- #8 Corrections: a setting decided later with an earlier effective day
  producing an adjustment posting
- #9 Multi-currency support
